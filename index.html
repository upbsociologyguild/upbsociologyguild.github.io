Welcome to the Guild, Social Butterfly!

                The UPB Sociology Guild is the only student-led academic organization under the 
                College of Social Sciences and in the University of the Philippines Baguio for 
                Sociology students and Sociology enthusiasts. Established on November 27, 2018, UPB SoGu 
                aims to: promote sociological thought and contribute to social change, strengthen Sociology 
                as a discipline, and advocate for Sociology as a way of life within the university 
                and to the wider public.
